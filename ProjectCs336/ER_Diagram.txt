================================================================================
                            RELATIONSHIPS SUMMARY
================================================================================

1. AIRLINE (1) ----< (N) AIRCRAFT
   - One airline can have many aircrafts
   - Each aircraft belongs to one airline

2. AIRPORT (1) ----< (N) FLIGHT ----> (1) AIRPORT
   - Many flights depart from one airport
   - Many flights arrive at one airport

3. AIRCRAFT (1) ----< (N) FLIGHT
   - One aircraft can operate many flights

4. FLIGHT (1) ----< (N) FLIGHT_INSTANCE
   - One flight template can have many daily instances

5. FLIGHT_INSTANCE (N) >----< (N) TICKET (through INCLUDES)
   - Many tickets can include many flight instances

6. CUSTOMER (1) ----< (N) RESERVATION
   - One customer can have many reservations

7. RESERVATION (1) ----< (N) TICKET
   - One reservation can have many tickets

8. CUSTOMER (N) >----< (N) FLIGHT_INSTANCE (through WAITS_FOR)
   - Many customers can wait for many flight instances

9. EMPLOYEE (1) ----< (N) RESERVATION (through MANAGES)
   - One employee can manage many reservations

================================================================================
                            CARDINALITY
================================================================================

- (1) : One
- (N) : Many
- (N:M) : Many to Many (requires junction table)
